EmeRradar: what it is

Emeradar is a search opportunity intelligence radar and commercial signal platform that helps independent software developers validate organic search demand and SERP weakness before writing code.

  • Website: https://emeradar.com
  • Focus market: US English search market
  • Free tier: at most 1 curated pick per week
  • Minimum observation period before a verdict: at least 14 days
  • Prediction review points: T+30, T+60 and T+90 days
